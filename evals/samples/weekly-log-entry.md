Supplier GSTIN: what the rules say
• The supplier's GSTIN is required for any expense above ₹2,000.00. A missing or invalid GSTIN is flagged for the approver.
• Policy checks never block a claim. They add flags (over item limit, over daily limit, missing receipt, missing or invalid GSTIN, GST mismatch, weekend, outside trip dates, possible duplicate) that the approving manager sees before deciding.
• A tax invoice issued by a registered person must show the name, address and GSTIN of the supplier, a consecutive serial number unique for the financial year (letters, numerals, hyphen or slash), and the date the invoice was issued.
• For an inter-State supply the invoice must show the place of supply with the State name, the delivery address if different, whether tax is payable on reverse charge, and the signature or digital signature of the supplier or an authorised representative.
