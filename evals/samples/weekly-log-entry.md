Supplier GSTIN Requirements
• The supplier's GSTIN is required for any expense above ₹2,000.00, and a missing or invalid GSTIN is flagged for the approver.
• Policy checks add a flag for missing or invalid GSTIN, which the approving manager sees before deciding.
• A tax invoice from a registered person must display the supplier's name, address and GSTIN, along with a unique serial number and issue date.
• For inter‑state supplies, the invoice must show the place of supply, delivery address if different, reverse‑charge status, and the supplier’s signature or digital signature.
